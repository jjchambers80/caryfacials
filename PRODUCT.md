# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Plain static HTML/CSS, no build step (user choice, 2026-11-20). Hosted on GitHub Pages at caryfacials.com; DNS at GoDaddy.

## Users
People in and around Cary, NC searching Google for a facial ("facials cary nc", "facial near me", "anti-aging facial cary"), mostly on phones. Most have never heard of Román Skin & Body. Their job: decide whether this spa offers the facial they want, at a price and length they accept, then book.

## Product Purpose
caryfacials.com is a local landing site that ranks for facial searches in Cary and sends visitors to book at Román Skin & Body. It has a home page about facials in Cary and four category pages (All & Sensitive Skin, Anti-Aging, Congested & Acne, Advanced Corrective). Success means booking clicks to https://www.romanskinandbody.com/pages/book-appointment and calls to the spa.

## Positioning
Facials performed by Betty Román at her independent Cary skin care studio. Site brand: "Cary Facials by Betty Román"; the business (NAP, schema, Google profile) stays Román Skin & Body. Betty is CIDESCO-accredited and co-founder of the North Carolina Esthetician Association. Treatments use Guinot (including the patented Hydradermie) and the Román Skin house line. Skin care only: no massage.

## Operating Context
- Bookings happen in Salon Iris via romanskinandbody.com; this site never takes bookings itself.
- Name, address and phone must match the Google Business Profile exactly: Román Skin & Body, 102-F Commonwealth Ct, Cary, NC 27511, (919) 463-9575.
- Hours (Google profile, 2026-11-19): Tue & Thu 10–7, Wed & Fri 10–6, Sat 10–4, Sun & Mon closed.
- Facial names, lengths and prices are copied from the romanskinandbody.com spa menu (Shopify templates `page.facials-all-sensitive`, `page.treatments-anti-aging`, `page.treatments-congested-acne`, `page.treatments-advanced-corrective`) and must be kept in sync when that menu changes.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 48-hour cancellation policy; late changes and no-shows are charged half the service price.
- Microneedling needs a consultation first. Skin Tight for the Neck & Jowls arrives December 2026.
- Acne facials work alongside medical care, not in place of it.

## Brand Commitments
- Site brand: **Cary Facials by Betty Román** (titles, wordmark, H1). Business name: **Román Skin & Body**, with the accent (NAP, schema, booking).
- Spa identity from romanskinandbody.com: Cormorant 500 headings, Jost body, olive #6D796E, ivory #F8F6F2, charcoal #3D4246.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real spa photos from romanskinandbody.com: studio entrance, reception, Betty's facial room, Betty Román portrait. A facial-in-progress image (`assets/spa-menu-facial.jpg` in the romanskinandbody project).
- Google rating/review count exists on the profile but is not copied here. No testimonials, awards or customer counts: don't invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Booking is one tap away on every page.
2. Plain facts over persuasion: what the facial does, how long, how much.
3. Every page reads as local to Cary, without keyword stuffing.
4. Never contradict the Google profile or the spa's own menu.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA (user requirement: ADA compliant). Mobile first.
