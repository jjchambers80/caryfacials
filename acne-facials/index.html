Questions about acne facials

Can a facial replace acne treatment from my doctor?

No. Facials work alongside medical care, not in place of it. For persistent or painful acne, see a dermatologist, and tell Betty about any treatment you are using.

Which acne facial should I book?

The Purifying Facial ($130, 60 min) rebalances oily skin and reduces shine. The Hydra Peel ($155, 45 min) exfoliates and smooths texture. The Hydraclean ($80, 45 min) is the quickest and least expensive, using a patented thermoclean electrode.

Are there packages?

Yes, for the Hydra Peel: 3 sessions for $450 or 6 sessions for $725.

What helps with acne marks and enlarged pores?

Two advanced corrective facials are aimed at this. The Brightening Facial with LED Light Therapy is anti-bacterial and suited to acne-prone skin, and the Bio Skin Renew peel targets acne, pigmentation spots and dilated pores.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. Later changes and missed appointments are charged half the service price.
