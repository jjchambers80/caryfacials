Questions about anti-aging facials

Which anti-aging facial lifts and firms the most?

The Lift Summum Facial ($165, 90 min) visibly lifts and tightens the skin. HydraLift with Microcurrent ($170, 90 min) uses microcurrent on the small muscles of the face and includes lymphatic drainage and a facial massage. Triple Collagen with Botoxal ($165, 75 min) adds elasticity with collagen and vitamins.

Does Triple Collagen with Botoxal involve injections?

No. It is a facial. Marine botoxal is one of the ingredients in the products, used alongside triple active collagen and vitamins.

Are there packages?

Yes, for the Hydra Peel: 3 sessions for $450 or 6 sessions for $725. A single Hydra Peel is $155 and takes 45 minutes.

Do you treat the neck and jawline?

Skin Tight for the Neck & Jowls arrives in December 2026. It is a 45-minute, noninvasive treatment ($170) that uses warm and cold contrast therapy to firm and tone the neck and jawline over time.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. Later changes and missed appointments are charged half the service price.
