Questions about corrective facials

Do I need a consultation for microneedling?

Yes. Microneedling Deluxe ($250, 75 min) needs a consultation first. It is a more invasive treatment, and you should expect about 24 hours of downtime.

Is LED light therapy suitable for sensitive skin or rosacea?

The studio recommends the Brightening Facial with LED Light Therapy for all skin types, including hypersensitive skin and rosacea. The LED light is FDA-approved and non-invasive.

How many Bio Skin Renew sessions do I need?

The studio recommends a course of 3 treatments, one a week, to follow the skin's cell-renewal rhythm. Each session is $175 and takes 90 minutes.

What does dermaplaning remove?

Dermaplane Deluxe removes surface dead skin cells and fine vellus hair (peach fuzz). It is followed by a brightening facial, and makeup goes on more smoothly afterward.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. Later changes and missed appointments are charged half the service price.
