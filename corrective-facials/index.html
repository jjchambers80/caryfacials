Questions about corrective facials

Do I need a consultation for microneedling?

Yes. Microneedling Deluxe ($250, 75 min) goes deeper than the other treatments here, so it starts with a consultation. Plan for about 24 hours of downtime afterward.

Is LED light therapy okay for sensitive skin or rosacea?

The studio recommends the Brightening Facial with LED Light Therapy for all skin types, including hypersensitive skin and rosacea. The LED light is FDA-approved and non-invasive.

How many Bio Skin Renew sessions do I need?

The studio recommends three sessions, one a week, to follow your skin's cell-renewal rhythm. Each session is $175 and takes 90 minutes.

What does dermaplaning remove?

Dermaplane Deluxe removes dead skin cells from the surface and the fine vellus hair known as peach fuzz. A brightening facial follows, and makeup goes on more smoothly afterward.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. Later changes and missed appointments are charged half the service price.
