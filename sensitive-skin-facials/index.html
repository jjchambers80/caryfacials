Questions about facials for sensitive skin

Which facial is best for sensitive skin?

For calming and hydration, choose the Kiss of Rose Facial ($165, 90 min), which uses microalgae and organic white rose to support the skin barrier, or the Hydra Summum Facial ($145, 60 min), which is rich in hyaluronic acid. The Sensitive Skin Treatment with Stem Cells ($160, 90 min) also suits skin that is starting to show age.

I've never had a facial. Where should I start?

The Román Deluxe Signature Facial ($160, 90 min) is designed to correct common imbalances such as oiliness, dehydration and redness. Every facial at the studio starts with your esthetician looking at your skin, so the treatment is adjusted to what they find.

What is a Hydradermie facial?

Hydradermie is Guinot's patented facial. Serums are chosen for your skin type, and a heated electrode works with Guinot's OZ2 cream to boost oxygenation. It costs $160 and takes 90 minutes.

Is there a shorter facial?

Yes. The Détoxygène Facial ($140) and the Hydra Summum Facial ($145) each take 60 minutes. Every other facial in this group takes 90 minutes.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment. Later changes and missed appointments are charged half the service price.
